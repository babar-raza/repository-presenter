- The 4 assembled Cells correction bundles: no dependency manifest; `gate.py` re-derives with current code and fails closed (A-21). Safe direction, but the plan has no regression guard; the Common-rules replacement above adds one.
- Can a live-anchored candidate reach propose without judge or human? No, if sealed: `proposal_gate_refusal` (honest review, measured no-op, judge, 30-point PASS) then registry `mode: full` (2 entries), the owner's merged authorization record bound to the candidate hash, the kill-switch variable, token provenance and a source recheck. - Disk and gateway: E: 97 percent full now (28 GB free); `present` leaves clones and transactions; 30 live runs is about 600 provider calls.
